LUMA

Digital Credit Infrastructure for responsible lending.

LUMA is a fintech platform designed to support the complete digital credit lifecycle — from borrower onboarding and KYC through credit decisioning, loan servicing, payments, recovery and reconciliation.

What LUMA does

Digital Origination Structured borrower onboarding and credit applications.
KYC & Eligibility Identity, eligibility and application controls.
Risk & Fraud Risk, fraud, score and trust decision layers.
Credit Decisioning Configurable approval tiers and decision logging.
Loan Lifecycle Contracts, installments, payments and recovery.
Reconciliation Payment, money movement and ledger reconciliation.

Current stage

LUMA 13.00.0 is a functional prototype with automated test coverage and a sandbox live-path acceptance flow.

Next milestone
Deploy a controlled real-money pilot with qualified partners, appropriate KYC, risk controls, legal structure and regulated money-movement infrastructure.
